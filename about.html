WHO WE ARE

Food That Brings People Together

At Antixor, we believe great food is more than just a meal. It is the heart of every gathering, the detail people remember, and the experience that brings everyone together.

We create carefully crafted menus using fresh ingredients, professional preparation, and beautiful presentation. From intimate private dinners to weddings, corporate events, and large celebrations, our team works closely with every client to create an experience that feels personal and unforgettable.

Every event is different, and that is exactly what we love. We listen to your ideas, understand your vision, and turn it into a dining experience designed especially for you.
